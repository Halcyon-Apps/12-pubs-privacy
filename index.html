12 Pubs of Christmas – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

12 Pubs of Christmas ("the app") helps friends plan a pub crawl: you name up to 12 pubs, set a date and times, and the app sends you reminders. This policy explains what information the app uses. The short version: there are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.

Who we are

The app is published by Halcyon-Apps. Contact: 12poc@halcyon-apps.com.

Age

The app mentions alcohol and is intended for adults of legal drinking age (18+). It is not directed at children.

What stays on your phone

  • Your plan: pub names, chosen town, date and start/end times, and the crawl name if you share or join one.
  • Your reminders. They are scheduled on your phone and are not sent through any server.

Deleting the app removes this information from your phone.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location while the app is open to suggest pubs and towns near you. It is optional: you can type a town instead. To find nearby places, your approximate coordinates and what you type are sent to the Photon search service (photon.komoot.io, operated by komoot and based on OpenStreetMap data). We do not store your location and do not send it to other users.

Sharing a crawl with friends

If you enter a crawl name and password and start the crawl, the app shares it with friends and stores this on our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU): the crawl name, a scrambled (hashed) version of the password you choose, and the plan (pub names and addresses, date and times). Anyone who knows the crawl name and password can view the plan. Only the organiser can change it. The organiser can stop sharing at any time, which deletes the shared plan from our server. To ask us to delete a shared crawl, email us at the address above with the crawl name.

Do not use a password you use anywhere else.

Buy the creators a beer (optional)

The app is free. You can choose to buy the creators a beer as a one-off thank-you. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card or payment details, and we do not receive your name or email address from the purchase. Nothing is unlocked by buying a beer. Refunds and receipts are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, name or email address to use the app.
  • We do not show adverts and do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Other apps and websites

Buttons such as Walk, Drive, Book a ride and Taxis near me open Google Maps, Uber or your web browser. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Notifications

The app asks permission to send reminders (for example "15 mins until the crawl starts", "10 mins to go", "5 mins to go" and "Time to leave"). If a crawl organiser changes or ends the plan, your app may also tell you with a notification. You can turn these off in your phone's settings.

Your choices

You can deny location or notification permission, stop sharing a crawl, or delete the app. If you are in the UK or EU you may also ask us what we hold about you and ask us to delete it, using the contact address above.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top.

Drink responsibly

Please drink responsibly, never drink and drive, and look after your friends.
